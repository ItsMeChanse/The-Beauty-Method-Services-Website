Questions

Which service should I book?

Not sure between gel, dip, and builder gel? Book the closest match and add a note. We’ll talk it through during the consult and adjust if there’s time.

Do I need soak-off removal?

If you’re coming in with gel, dip, or extensions from somewhere else, add Soak Off Removal so there’s time to take them off gently.

Can I bring inspiration photos?

Please do. Add a note when you book, and bring the photos on your phone.

How do I make my set last?

Wear gloves for cleaning and dishes, use cuticle oil daily, and don’t use your nails as tools. Book your fill or next set before things start lifting.
